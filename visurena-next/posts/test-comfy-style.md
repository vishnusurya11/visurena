# Welcome to the New Blog Design

This is a test post to showcase the new **Comfy.org-inspired** blog styling. The design focuses on readability, clean typography, and beautiful presentation of images and videos.

## Key Features

The new design includes:

- **Soft dark background** (#0a0a0a) - easier on the eyes than pure black
- **Readable serif font** for body text (Georgia)
- **Crisp white headings** for excellent contrast
- **Generous spacing** - content has room to breathe
- **Beautiful image presentation** - full-width, rounded corners, shadows

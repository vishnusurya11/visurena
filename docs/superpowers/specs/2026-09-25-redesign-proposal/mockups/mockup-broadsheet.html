The casebook is the paper trail: the Lauriston Garden floor plan, the two pills, the wedding ring and the advertisement placed in the evening papers. Twenty-two documents, each linked to the chapter it belongs to.

Open any document to see the frame it appears in.

Doyle’s novel is two books stapled together: a London detective story and a Utah revenge story. The adaptation keeps the seam. Episodes 1–7 follow Watson’s reminiscences; episodes 8–12 leave London entirely for the Great Alkali Plain; 13 and 14 bring the two halves back to Baker Street.

Every frame was generated from a written shot list and chosen by one person. Dialogue is Doyle’s wherever it could be, trimmed for a ten-minute chapter. Nothing was added to the plot.

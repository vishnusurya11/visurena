// HOW BLOG WORKS

Blog is the room where we publish what we're doing and why. Build logs. Architecture posts. Transparency reports — what the audience asked for, what we shipped, what we declined, why.

If you're using audience taste as your production input, you owe the audience an account of how you used it. Blog is that account, in public, in writing.

Phase 2+ commitment: monthly transparency report. User counts, opt-out rates, what the engine got right, where it failed. Where the money went. Public dashboard at /transparency.

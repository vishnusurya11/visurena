Six dark directions.
One brand DNA.

Each direction has a parent palette plus 6 derived section accents (Search · For You · Watch · Read · Play · Listen · Research). Master accent stays glued to interactive elements; section accents shift per page so each room feels distinct without breaking brand cohesion.

// Nav update · "Discover" → "For You"
SEARCH · FOR YOU · WATCH · READ · PLAY · LISTEN · RESEARCH

"Discover" was generic and tech-flavored. "For You" frames the tab as recommendation — what the engine surfaces based on your taste signal. Familiar from Apple TV+/Spotify; instantly readable. Easy to swap to PICKS / TONIGHT / TASTE / MATCHED if you prefer.

Per-section accents — derived from each parent

Master accent stays glued to interactive elements (button fill, link hover, focus ring, active nav indicator). Each section gets its own surface accent — eyebrow, page tint, active-nav indicator — derived from the master so Watch ≠ Read ≠ Play ≠ Listen ≠ Research without breaking brand cohesion.

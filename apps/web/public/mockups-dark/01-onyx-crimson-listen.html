Listen is the first medium expansion after the platform proves the loop with text and games. Music is short — a track ships in days, not months — so it’s the cheapest way to prove the demand-first thesis.

Tracks come from search clusters. Someone hunts for ‘electronic dream-pop with a queer protagonist.’ Twenty more do the same. The cluster becomes a brief. The brief becomes a track.

Phase 4a target: a single → an EP → an album. Editorial picks the genre. AI handles production under direction. The audience hears what they asked for.

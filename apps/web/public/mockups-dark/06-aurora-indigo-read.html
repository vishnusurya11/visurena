// HOW STORY WORKS

Story is where the demand-driven engine ships its longest form. Novels are serialized — chapter by chapter — so the audience can react before the next one is written.

Editorial holds the spine: where the story’s going, what doesn’t move. The crowd shapes texture: scenes, beats, an image lodged in too many heads to ignore. Crowd-steered, not crowd-written.

Phase 3 target: one full novel completed, 8–10 chapters. The first chapter ships when the demand cluster passes 200 distinct taste-inputs.

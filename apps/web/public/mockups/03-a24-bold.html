The Thesis · 01

Most platforms ship what's already made. We ship what you ask for.

Demand-first. Streaming caught up to taste; it never caught up to demand. Catalogs are still finished cans of film, lined up to be browsed. We invert it. The home page is not a wall of posters — it's a brief box. You name the thing that hit you, and why. That signal becomes the production order. The platform is the studio's intake desk, not its lobby.

Crowd-steered, not crowd-written. Audiences are great at telling you what's missing and terrible at writing the script. We aggregate the briefs into demand clusters, editorial sets the angle, AI does the heavy lift, and a human director signs the cut. The crowd decides the destination. The studio decides the road. That is the line we will not cross.

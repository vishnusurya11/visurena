Six directions for the brand.

Each is a complete home-page mockup grounded in the BRD: hero IS the taste-capture intake, single signature accent, no "Coming Soon" placeholders. Open them side-by-side and the right one will surface fast.

Per-section accents — derived, not random

For each direction, the master accent stays glued to interactive elements (verb color: button, link, focus). Each section gets its own surface accent — eyebrow, page tint, active-nav indicator — derived from the master so Watch, Read, Play, Listen, and Research feel distinct without breaking brand cohesion.
